Welcome to the Warming Room Project

Your central hub for managing warming room operations, volunteers, and community proposals. Choose from our integrated systems below to get started.

👥

Volunteer Coordination System

Comprehensive volunteer management platform for warming room operations. Manage schedules, track volunteers, coordinate shifts, and ensure reliable coverage.

Key Features:

  • 📅 Automated shift scheduling
  • 📱 Multi-channel communications
  • 👨‍🏫 Skills & training tracking
  • 📊 Real-time coverage dashboard
  • 🔄 Backup volunteer management
  • 📈 Volunteer hours & reporting

Community Proposals System

Collaborative platform for community members to submit, discuss, and manage proposals for improving warming room services and operations.

Key Features:

  • 📝 Proposal submission & management
  • 💬 Community discussion & feedback
  • 🔄 Status tracking & updates
  • 🏷️ Categorization & filtering
  • 📋 Implementation tracking
  • 🤝 Stakeholder collaboration
