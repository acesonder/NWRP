# Volunteer Coordination System - Features

## Core Features

### 1. Volunteer Management 👥

#### Volunteer Registration
- **Self-Registration**: Online form for new volunteers
- **Admin Registration**: Staff can add volunteers manually
- **Contact Information**: Multiple phone numbers, email, emergency contacts
- **Preference Tracking**: Communication method preferences, availability windows
- **Status Management**: Active, pending, inactive, suspended statuses

#### Skills & Qualifications
- **Skill Categories**: First aid, languages, medical, mental health, leadership
- **Certification Tracking**: Expiration dates and renewal reminders
- **Training Records**: Completion status and dates
- **Background Checks**: Status and completion tracking
- **Experience Levels**: New, experienced, team lead capabilities

#### Availability Management
- **Weekly Schedules**: Day/time availability preferences
- **Blackout Dates**: Vacation and unavailable periods
- **Preferred Shifts**: Ranking of shift preferences
- **Maximum Hours**: Weekly and daily hour limits
- **Backup Status**: Willing to serve as backup volunteer

### 2. Shift Scheduling 📅

#### Shift Templates
- **Standard Shifts**: Evening (6pm-10pm), Night (10pm-2am), Early Morning (2am-6am), Cleanup (6am-9am)
- **Custom Shifts**: Create non-standard time blocks
- **Staffing Requirements**: Minimum and maximum volunteers per shift
- **Skill Requirements**: Required certifications or experience levels
- **Role Definitions**: Volunteer, shift lead, backup assignments

#### Automated Scheduling
- **Template Application**: Apply shift templates across date ranges
- **Volunteer Matching**: Automatic assignment based on availability and skills
- **Conflict Detection**: Prevent double-booking and over-scheduling
- **Backup Assignment**: Automatic backup volunteer allocation
- **Load Balancing**: Distribute shifts fairly among volunteers

#### Manual Override
- **Direct Assignment**: Manually assign specific volunteers
- **Swap Requests**: Facilitate volunteer shift exchanges
- **Last-Minute Changes**: Quick reassignment for cancellations
- **Emergency Coverage**: Rapid response for urgent needs
- **Shift Modifications**: Adjust times, requirements, or staffing

### 3. Communication System 📱

#### Multi-Channel Notifications
- **Email**: Formatted HTML messages with embedded links
- **SMS**: Brief text messages for urgent communications
- **Phone**: Automated voice calls for critical alerts
- **In-App**: Dashboard notifications and alerts
- **Push Notifications**: Mobile-friendly instant alerts

#### Message Types
- **Shift Reminders**: 24-hour advance notifications
- **Schedule Changes**: Immediate alerts for modifications
- **Weather Alerts**: Activation triggers and updates  
- **Emergency Communications**: Critical operational updates
- **General Announcements**: Program updates and information

#### Automated Workflows
- **Reminder Schedule**: Configurable advance notice periods
- **Escalation Procedures**: Progressive notification for unfilled shifts
- **Confirmation Requests**: Two-way communication for attendance
- **Follow-Up Messages**: Post-shift feedback and thank you notes
- **Training Reminders**: Certification renewal notifications

### 4. Operational Dashboard 📊

#### Real-Time Status
- **Coverage Overview**: Visual shift fill status
- **Active Volunteers**: Currently on-duty personnel
- **Pending Issues**: Unfilled shifts and alerts
- **Weather Integration**: Current conditions and forecasts

- **No-Show Rate**: Volunteer attendance reliability
- **Response Time**: Speed of shift filling after activation
- **Volunteer Retention**: Long-term participation rates
- **Guest Satisfaction**: Service quality feedback

#### Quick Actions
- **Emergency Assignment**: One-click volunteer calling
- **Broadcast Alerts**: Immediate system-wide communications
- **Shift Creation**: Rapid additional shift scheduling
- **Status Updates**: Real-time operational changes
- **Incident Reporting**: Quick issue documentation
